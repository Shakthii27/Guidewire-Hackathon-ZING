Coverage &
Exclusions
Policy document · Effective from your activation date
✓ What Zing Covers
✓Income loss from heatwave — WBGT ≥ 35°C sustained for 2+ hours in your registered zone
✓Income loss from flash floods — rainfall ≥ 40mm/hr causing zone waterlogging confirmed by IMD
✓Income loss from severe AQI — AQI ≥ 450 (Hazardous) for 3+ hours in your active delivery pin code
✓Payouts are parametric — triggered automatically by verified sensor data, not your self-reported claim
⚠️ Zing is parametric income insurance — payouts trigger only when objective sensor thresholds are crossed. Events outside these triggers are not covered, regardless of their impact on your ability to deliver.
🚫 Events That Never Trigger a Payout
🌡
Heat Below Threshold
If WBGT stays below 35°C, no heatwave payout is issued — even if you feel too hot to work or a local advisory is issued. The parametric trigger is objective and non-negotiable.
THRESHOLD
🌧
Rain Without Flood Trigger
Ordinary monsoon rain that doesn't cause IMD-confirmed waterlogging or breach the 40mm/hr threshold is not covered, even if orders slow down or roads get wet.
THRESHOLD
💨
Moderate Air Pollution
AQI between 300–449 (Very Poor) does not trigger a payout. Only Hazardous AQI ≥ 450 sustained for 3+ hours in your pin code qualifies.
THRESHOLD
🤒
Illness, Injury & Medical Events
Personal illness, accidents, hospitalisation, or physical injury — including those caused by weather — are not covered. Zing insures income lost to environmental disruption, not personal health events. Seek a separate health or accident policy.
NOT COVERED
🛵
Vehicle Breakdown or Damage
Mechanical failure, tyre puncture, accident repair, or theft of your delivery vehicle is not covered. These require a separate motor or vehicle insurance policy.
NOT COVERED
📵
Platform Suspension or Deactivation
If Zomato or Swiggy suspends, bans, or deactivates your account, Zing does not cover the resulting income loss. Coverage requires an active, in-good-standing platform account.
NOT COVERED
🧨
Civil Unrest, Strikes & Bandhs
Income loss from curfews, political protests, bandhs, riots, or civil disturbances is excluded. These are classified as force majeure events outside Zing's parametric model.
NOT COVERED
🏗
Road Closures & Infrastructure Issues
Planned or emergency road closures, metro construction disruptions, traffic diversions, or signal failures that reduce delivery opportunities are not covered.
NOT COVERED
⚡
Power Outages & Network Downtime
Platform app downtime, internet outages, or electricity failures that prevent you from accepting orders are not a covered event under Zing's parametric triggers.
NOT COVERED
🎭
Intentional Non-Work & Self-Exclusion
Choosing not to work for personal reasons — festivals, rest days, family commitments — does not qualify, even if a weather trigger is active. Morning check-in must be completed by 9 AM to establish shift intent.
NOT COVERED
🛡 Fraud & Misrepresentation Exclusions
📍
GPS Spoofing or Zone Mismatch
If your device location doesn't match your registered delivery zone during the disruption window, the claim is automatically voided. Mock-location apps are detected and result in immediate account suspension.
FRAUD
💸
Parallel Income During Disruption
If platform earnings data shows you completed deliveries on another app (Swiggy, Porter, etc.) during the disruption window, the claim is rejected. Zing pays for lost income only — not double-income periods.
FRAUD
🪪
Identity Mismatch
Payouts are locked to your biometric-verified identity. Accounts shared across individuals, fake Rider IDs, or failed face-match during payout are grounds for permanent claim denial and account termination.
FRAUD
📋
Zing is a parametric micro-insurance product regulated under IRDAI guidelines for weather-index insurance. All exclusions comply with IRDAI's standard uninsurable events framework. For disputes, contact our grievance officer at grievance@zinginsure.in within 15 days of a denied payout. Full policy wording available in the app's Documents section.

Air Temperature 44°C
Relative Humidity 62%
Wind Speed 8 km/h
